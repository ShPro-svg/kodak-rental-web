1. RENTAL RATES & PACKAGE

• Weekdays (Monday - Thursday): RM18/day

Rental Package Includes: 1x Kodak Camera, 1x Battery, 1x SD Card, 1x SD Card Reader (Type-C / Lightning), 1x Hand Strap, 1x Camera Pouch / Bag.

Security Deposit: A refundable security deposit of RM20 is required for every rental. The deposit will be fully refunded once the camera and all accessories are returned in good working condition, complete, and without damage.

2. BOOKING CONFIRMATION & CANCELLATION POLICY

Booking Confirmation: A booking is only confirmed upon receipt of full payment. Bookings operate on a First Pay, First Serve basis.

Cancellation Policy:
• More than 24 hours before the rental period: 100% refund.
• Less than 24 hours before the rental period: The RM20 deposit is non-refundable, while the rental fee will be refunded.

3. SECURITY DOCUMENTS (MANDATORY)

For security and record-keeping purposes, renters must provide one of the following: Student ID Card; or Front side of National Identity Card (IC). All submitted documents will remain confidential and will only be used in cases involving loss, theft, or rental-related disputes.

4. PICK-UP & RETURN

Pick-Up: The condition of the camera and all accessories will be inspected jointly by both the owner and renter during collection.

Return: The renter must return the camera on the agreed date and time.

Late Return Fee: A late fee of RM5 per hour will be charged for any delay. If the renter anticipates any delay, they must notify the owner as soon as possible. Waiving of late fees is subject to the owner's discretion and dependent on future booking availability.

5. DAMAGE, LOSS & LIABILITY

Camera Care: This camera is not waterproof and must not be exposed to water, sand, excessive impact, or any conditions that may cause damage through negligence.

Loss or Damage of Accessories: Replacement costs will first be deducted from the security deposit. If replacement costs exceed the RM20 deposit, the renter agrees to pay the remaining balance.
Replacement Charges: SD Card Reader: RM15 | Battery: RM25 | SD Card: RM25.

Critical Damage or Loss of Camera: In the event of loss of the camera, theft due to renter negligence, or severe damage rendering the camera unusable, the renter agrees to bear the full repair cost or replacement cost based on the current market value (Camera Value: ~RM300).

6. RENTER'S RESPONSIBILITY DURING RENTAL PERIOD

The renter is fully responsible for the camera and all rented accessories throughout the entire rental period, from the moment of collection until they are returned to the owner. If the camera or accessories are lent, shared, transferred, used, or held by any third party including friends, family members, partners, or any other individual, the original renter shall remain fully responsible for any loss, theft, damage, late return, or any issues arising from the use of the rented equipment. The owner will only deal with the renter named in this agreement and shall not be responsible for pursuing claims against any third party who used the rented equipment.

7. FILE MANAGEMENT & PRIVACY

The renter is responsible for transferring all photos and videos to their own device before returning the camera. Upon return, the memory card will be fully formatted. No copies of photos or videos will be retained by the owner. The owner shall not be responsible for any data loss resulting from files not transferred before the camera is returned.

TERMA & SYARAT RASMI SEWAAN DIGICAM KODAK

1. KADAR SEWAAN & PAKEJ

• Hari Biasa (Isnin - Khamis): RM18/hari
• Hari Biasa (4 hari dan ke atas): RM15/hari
• Hujung Minggu (Jumaat - Ahad): RM25/hari
• Pakej Mingguan (7 hari berturut-turut): RM100

Pakej Sewaan Termasuk: 1x Kamera Kodak, 1x Bateri, 1x Kad SD, 1x Pembaca Kad SD (Type-C / Lightning), 1x Tali Tangan, 1x Pouch / Beg Kamera.

Deposit Keselamatan: Deposit keselamatan sebanyak RM20 diperlukan untuk setiap sewaan. Deposit akan dipulangkan sepenuhnya sebaik sahaja kamera dan semua aksesori dipulangkan dalam keadaan baik, lengkap, dan tanpa sebarang kerosakan.

2. PENGESAHAN TEMPAHAN & POLISI PEMBATALAN

Pengesahan Tempahan: Tempahan hanya disahkan setelah bayaran penuh diterima. Tempahan beroperasi mengikut prinsip 'First Pay, First Serve'.

Polisi Pembatalan:
• Lebih 24 jam sebelum tempoh sewaan: Pulangan penuh 100%.
• Kurang 24 jam sebelum tempoh sewaan: Deposit RM20 tidak akan dipulangkan, manakala yuran sewaan akan dikembalikan.

3. DOKUMEN KESELAMATAN (WAJIB)

Untuk tujuan keselamatan dan rekod, penyewa mesti menyediakan salah satu daripada berikut: Kad ID Pelajar (Kad Matrik); atau Gambar bahagian depan Kad Pengenalan (IC). Dokumen tersebut mestilah dipalang dan ditanda dengan: "UNTUK SEWAAN DIGICAM SAHAJA". Semua dokumen yang diserahkan adalah sulit dan hanya akan dirujuk jika berlaku kes kehilangan, kecurian, atau pertikaian sewaan.

4. PENGAMBILAN & PEMULANGAN

Pengambilan: Keadaan kamera dan semua aksesori akan diperiksa bersama oleh pemilik dan penyewa semasa sesi penyerahan.

Pemulangan: Penyewa mesti memulangkan kamera pada tarikh dan masa yang telah dipersetujui.

Yuran Kelewatan: Caj lewat sebanyak RM5 sejam akan dikenakan untuk sebarang kelewatan. Jika penyewa menjangkakan sebarang kelewatan, mereka mesti memaklumkan pemilik secepat mungkin. Pengecualian yuran lewat adalah tertakluk kepada budi bicara pemilik dan bergantung pada ketersediaan tempahan seterusnya.

5. KEROSAKAN, KEHILANGAN & LIABILITI

Penjagaan Kamera: Kamera ini tidak kalis air dan tidak boleh didedahkan kepada air, pasir, impak terjatuh yang kuat, atau sebarang keadaan yang boleh menyebabkan kerosakan akibat kecuaian.

Kehilangan atau Kerosakan Aksesori: Kos penggantian akan ditolak daripada deposit keselamatan terlebih dahulu. Jika kos melebihi deposit RM20, penyewa bersetuju untuk membayar baki tersebut.
Caj Penggantian: Pembaca Kad SD: RM15 | Bateri: RM25 | Kad SD: RM25.

Kerosakan Kritikal atau Kehilangan Kamera: Jika berlaku kehilangan kamera, kecurian akibat kecuaian penyewa, atau kerosakan teruk yang menyebabkan kamera tidak dapat digunakan, penyewa bersetuju untuk menanggung kos pembaikan penuh atau kos penggantian berdasarkan nilai pasaran semasa (Nilai Kamera: ~RM300).

Penyewa bertanggungjawab sepenuhnya ke atas kamera dan semua aksesori yang disewa sepanjang tempoh sewaan, dari saat pengambilan sehingga ia dipulangkan kepada pemilik. Pemilik hanya akan berurusan dengan penyewa yang dinamakan dalam perjanjian ini dan tidak bertanggungjawab untuk menuntut ganti rugi daripada pihak ketiga.

Penyewa bertanggungjawab untuk memindahkan semua foto dan video ke peranti mereka sendiri sebelum memulangkan kamera. Selepas pemulangan, kad memori akan diformat sepenuhnya.
